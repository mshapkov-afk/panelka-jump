Privacy Policy

Application: Panelka Jump  |  Effective Date: September 19, 2026  |  Developer: Balkan Games Studio

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Panelka Jump (the "Application"), developed and published by Balkan Games Studio ("we", "us", or "our").

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the Application, how that information is used, which third-party services are involved, and how online multiplayer and in-game communication features operate.

1. Accounts and Player Data

Panelka Jump can be played without creating a registered player account. Account registration and sign-in are optional and are provided through GDevelop Services.

If you choose to create or use a player account, GDevelop Services may process information required to authenticate you and maintain your player session.

  • Authentication: Player authentication and account sessions are handled through GDevelop Services.
  • Player Profile: Certain profile information, such as your chosen player name or nickname, may be used within online features of the game.
  • Leaderboards: Online leaderboard functionality is provided through GDevelop Services. A player's chosen nickname and submitted score may be displayed on the game's leaderboard.
  • Cloud Save: For registered players, certain game data may be stored in Google Firebase Firestore so that progress can be restored or synchronized.
  • Locally Stored Data: Some game settings and progress may also be stored locally on the player's device.

Cloud-saved information may include gameplay-related data such as high scores, collected in-game currency, unlocked cosmetic items, selected characters or cosmetics, achievements, statistics, settings, and similar game progress information.

2. Multiplayer and In-Game Chat

Panelka Jump includes online multiplayer functionality that allows players to participate in multiplayer sessions with other players. Multiplayer functionality is provided using GDevelop multiplayer and online services.

When using multiplayer features, certain information may be transmitted or made visible to other participants in the same multiplayer lobby or game session. This may include:

  • your player name or nickname;
  • selected avatar, character, cosmetics, or similar profile elements;
  • gameplay status and multiplayer session information;
  • scores and other information required to determine multiplayer results;
  • multiplayer selections or actions required to operate the match;
  • predefined quick-chat messages; and
  • messages that you voluntarily enter into the in-game text chat.

In-game chat messages are user-provided content and are transmitted as necessary to allow communication with other participants in the relevant multiplayer session.

Balkan Games Studio does not intentionally include the contents of multiplayer chat messages in the player's normal cloud-save data, leaderboard data, or permanent game-progress records.

The in-game chat is intended primarily for temporary communication during multiplayer use. Third-party online service providers may, however, technically process or temporarily handle information required to transmit multiplayer communications in accordance with their own systems, security practices, and privacy policies.

Please Do Not Share Sensitive Personal Information

Players should not use the in-game chat to share passwords, financial information, home addresses, telephone numbers, private contact information, or other sensitive personal data. Information voluntarily sent to another player may be seen by that player.

If you contact Balkan Games Studio regarding inappropriate behaviour, abuse, or another multiplayer issue, we may process the information that you voluntarily provide to us for the purpose of reviewing and responding to your request.

3. Advertising

Panelka Jump uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including optional rewarded advertisements.

AdMob and related Google services may process technical information such as device information, IP address, advertising identifiers, ad interaction information, and similar data needed to provide, measure, secure, and improve advertising services.

Depending on your region, device settings, age-related requirements, and consent choices, advertising may be personalized or non-personalized.

More information about how Google uses information for advertising can be found at: Google Advertising Policies .

4. In-App Purchases

Panelka Jump may offer optional in-app purchases. Purchases on Android are processed through Google Play Billing.

Balkan Games Studio does not directly receive or store your payment card number or other complete payment credentials. Payment processing is handled by the relevant application store.

The Application may receive information necessary to determine whether a purchase has been completed and which in-game entitlement should be granted.

5. Third-Party Services

Panelka Jump uses third-party services that may process information under their own privacy policies.

  • GDevelop Services – player authentication, player profiles, leaderboards, multiplayer lobbies, online multiplayer functionality, multiplayer communication, and associated online game services.
    GDevelop Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase / Firestore – backend cloud storage for game progress and related player data.
    Firebase Privacy Information
  • Google AdMob – advertising and rewarded advertisements.
    Google Advertising Policies
  • Google Play – application distribution, in-app billing, licensing, and related store functionality.
    Google Privacy Policy

6. Device Permissions

Panelka Jump does not intentionally request sensitive runtime permissions such as access to your camera, microphone, precise location, or contacts for normal gameplay.

Internet connectivity is required for certain online features, including authentication, leaderboards, cloud synchronization, advertising, multiplayer functionality, and in-game multiplayer communication.

7. Children's Privacy and Online Communication

Panelka Jump is intended for a general audience. We do not intentionally ask players to provide unnecessary personal information.

The Application may contain online multiplayer and text-chat functionality that allows communication between players. Players should not share personal or sensitive information through these features.

Some third-party services used by the Application may process limited technical information necessary to provide the game, advertisements, authentication, purchases, multiplayer features, or other online functionality.

Where required, age-related restrictions, consent requirements, and applicable platform policies are followed when providing advertising and online services.

If a parent or legal guardian believes that a child has provided personal information that should be removed, they may contact us using the details below.

8. How We Use Information

Information processed through Panelka Jump may be used where necessary to:

  • provide and operate the game;
  • authenticate player accounts;
  • save and restore game progress;
  • provide online leaderboards;
  • create and operate multiplayer lobbies and matches;
  • transmit multiplayer gameplay information;
  • enable communication between multiplayer participants;
  • process in-app purchases and game entitlements;
  • provide and measure advertising;
  • maintain security and prevent fraud or abuse;
  • respond to player support or privacy requests; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

9. Data Retention and Account Deletion

Account and Data Deletion

Players who use a registered account may request deletion of their account-related game data and cloud-saved progress. To request deletion, contact panelkajump@gmail.com .

We may retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Application, comply with legal requirements, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, and enforce applicable agreements.

Multiplayer chat content is not intentionally stored by Balkan Games Studio as part of normal cloud-save or leaderboard records. Information required for transmission may be processed temporarily by the online multiplayer service used by the Application.

Information processed independently by third-party providers may also be subject to those providers' own retention policies.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your country or region and applicable data protection law, you may have rights regarding your personal information. These may include the right to:

  • request information about how your personal data is processed;
  • request access to personal data associated with you;
  • request correction of inaccurate personal data;
  • request deletion of personal data where applicable;
  • request restriction of certain processing;
  • object to certain processing where applicable;
  • request data portability where applicable;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; and
  • submit a complaint to an appropriate data protection authority.

To submit a privacy-related request concerning information controlled by Balkan Games Studio, contact us at panelkajump@gmail.com .

Information controlled independently by third-party providers may need to be managed through the privacy or account-management tools provided by those services.

11. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information associated with Panelka Jump and use established third-party platforms for authentication, cloud storage, multiplayer functionality, advertising, and payment processing.

However, no electronic storage, online communication, or internet transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Players should therefore avoid transmitting confidential or sensitive personal information through the multiplayer chat.

12. International Processing

Third-party service providers used by Panelka Jump may process information on servers located in different countries.

Their processing is governed by their respective privacy policies and applicable data protection requirements.

13. Legal Bases for Processing

Where applicable data protection law requires a legal basis for processing personal data, information may be processed on one or more of the following bases:

  • Performance of a service or contract: where processing is necessary to provide game functionality, player accounts, multiplayer sessions, cloud saves, leaderboards, or purchases requested by the player.
  • Consent: where consent is required, for example for certain advertising or related technologies.
  • Legitimate interests: where appropriate for security, fraud prevention, service operation, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, or improvement of the Application, provided those interests are not overridden by applicable privacy rights.
  • Legal obligations: where processing is required to comply with applicable laws, regulations, legal requests, or financial obligations.
