This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Panelka Jump (hereinafter referred to as "the Application"), developed by mshapkov-afk ("we", "us", or "our"). 1. Information Collection and Use

We respect player privacy. Panelka Jump is designed as a standalone mobile game. We do not require users to create an account, register, or provide personally identifiable information (such as your full name, home address, or phone number) to play the game.

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers (e.g., IDFA for iOS, GAID for Android).

  • Network & IP Address: Coarse location data derived from IP addresses for ad geographic targeting.

The third-party service providers utilized by the Application include:

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Application does not explicitly use traditional browser cookies. However, third-party advertising or analytics SDKs incorporated into the Application may use mobile identifiers or similar technologies to collect information for personalized advertising or reporting.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

We are committed to protecting children's privacy. The Application does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain EU jurisdictions). If a parent or guardian becomes aware that their child has provided personal information without consent, please contact us immediately so we can promptly delete such information.

5. Data Safety & Data Deletion Rights

Google Play & App Store Data Rights Statement

Because Panelka Jump does not collect, store, or process user accounts or personal profiles on external developer servers, we do not maintain personal user records. All local game progress (e.g., high scores) is stored locally on your physical device or securely via Google Play Games / Apple Game Center. You may clear all local data at any time by uninstalling the Application or clearing its app data in your device settings.
