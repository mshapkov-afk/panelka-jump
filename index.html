Privacy Policy

Application: Panelka Jump  |  Effective Date: August 29, 2026  |  Developer: mshapkov-afk

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Panelka Jump (hereinafter referred to as "the Application"), developed by mshapkov-afk ("we", "us", or "our"). This document explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you download, install, and play our game via Google Play Store or Apple App Store.

1. Account Registration & User Data (Optional)

Panelka Jump can be played as a standalone game without creating an account. Account creation and sign-in are entirely optional and provided via GDevelop Services (including Google Sign-In, Apple Sign-In, or Email authentication) for players who wish to sync their progress across devices.

  • Authentication & Profile: When you register via GDevelop Services, an authentication token and associated user account are generated and linked with Firebase Authentication to secure your identity.
  • Cloud Save & Storage: For registered users, game progress, stats, and achievements are securely stored and synced in the cloud using Google Cloud / Firebase Firestore. For guest players, progress is saved locally on the device.

2. Data Collected Automatically & Third-Party Services

To deliver ads, process purchases, and ensure game functionality, the Application integrates third-party SDKs that may automatically collect certain technical data, including:

  • Device & Network Information: Device model, operating system version, unique mobile identifiers (e.g., GAID / Advertising ID), and IP address.
  • In-App Purchases: Billing transactions are handled directly and securely by the Google Play Billing system. We do not collect or store your credit card details or financial information.

The third-party service providers utilized by the Application include:

  • GDevelop Services: Used to handle player account creation, login authentication, and player profile sessions. (GDevelop Privacy Policy)
  • Google Firebase & Firestore: Used for secure user authentication management and backend cloud database storage (Firestore) for save files and game progress. (Firebase Privacy Policy)
  • AdMob (Google): Used for delivering in-game advertisements. (Google AdMob Policy)
  • Google Play Services: Used for achievements, leaderboards, and store services. (Google Privacy Policy)

3. Application Permissions

Panelka Jump does not request any sensitive runtime device permissions (such as Camera, Microphone, Location, or Contacts).

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

The Application is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain EU jurisdictions). If a parent or guardian becomes aware that their child has provided personal information without consent, please contact us immediately to have such data removed.

5. Data Safety & Data Deletion Rights

User Rights & Account/Data Deletion

In compliance with Google Play Developer Policies, all players who choose to create an account have the right to request the deletion of their account and associated cloud data stored in Firebase/Firestore. If you wish to permanently delete your registered account token and cloud-saved progress, please send a deletion request to panelkajump@gmail.com with your registered email details.

6. Security

We value your trust in providing your data. We strive to use commercially acceptable technical measures to protect your information, though no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective immediately upon posting to this URL.
